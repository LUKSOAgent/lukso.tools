## Near Field Circuit - Phygital Authentication

**Article:** https://medium.com/lukso/meet-near-field-circuit-a-new-era-of-phygital-art-and-authentication-built-on-lukso-cbb151b7b71b

### What is Near Field Circuit?
Near Field Circuit is a **phygital** (physical + digital) authentication system built on LUKSO that bridges physical items with their digital identities using NFC technology.

### How It Works
- Physical items contain NFC chips
- Scanning the NFC chip links to the item's Universal Profile on LUKSO
- Creates verifiable digital provenance for physical goods
- Uses LUKSO's LSP standards for asset representation

### Problem It Solves
- **Authentication:** Verifies authenticity of physical items
- **Provenance:** Tracks ownership history on-chain
- **Digital twins:** Creates persistent digital representations of physical assets
- **Brand protection:** Anti-counterfeiting through blockchain verification

### Other Phygital Projects on LUKSO
1. **Tuszy/phygital** (GitHub repository) - Open source phygital toolkit
2. **Family Labs** - UniversalGoods Protocol on LUKSO for physical-digital commerce
3. **LUKSO Phygital T-shirts** - Showcased at Berlin Fashion Week

### NFC Blockchain Comparison
Other notable NFC blockchain projects include OriginTrail, IYK, and various supply chain solutions. Near Field Circuit differentiates through deep LUKSO integration with Universal Profiles.
